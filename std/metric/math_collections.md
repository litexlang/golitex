# Metric spaces and maps

A MetricSpace<Ambient> supplies an actual carrier contained in Ambient and a
real distance on its square. Nonnegativity, identity, separation, symmetry and
the triangle inequality are direct structure laws. Empty carriers are allowed.
No arbitrary point or representative is selected.

Open balls use positive real radii; closed balls admit zero radius. Both are
actual set-valued functions. Reverse triangle, nested and disjoint balls and
interior subballs follow directly from the metric laws and real order.

Lipschitz continuity includes a nonnegative constant, permitting constant maps.
Pointwise continuity uses the ordinary epsilon-delta condition with both points
in the actual carrier. The checked Lipschitz-to-continuity proof chooses
delta = epsilon/(L+1), including L=0 without dividing by L.

Dependency spine: real order -> metric structure -> distance estimates ->
balls -> separation; independently function spaces and metrics -> Lipschitz
maps -> composition and epsilon-delta continuity. These are definition,
well-definedness and proof edges. A topology generated by the balls
and compactness remain later work. Metric sequence/Cauchy/completeness and
fixed-point theory are now provided by the separate `metric_sequences` module,
which depends on this module and `real_series` rather than widening this module's
imports. The real-line
absolute-difference example must be constructed and checked rather than assumed.
The metric, ball and map spine above is materialized in main.lit. The README
records strict source and imported-consumer evidence and later-work boundaries.
