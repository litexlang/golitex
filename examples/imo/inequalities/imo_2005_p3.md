# IMO 2005 — Problem 3

Fifth-power rational inequality.

## Problem

Let $x,y,z$ be positive real numbers satisfying $xyz\ge1$. Prove
\[
\frac{x^5-x^2}{x^5+y^2+z^2}
+\frac{y^5-y^2}{x^2+y^5+z^2}
+\frac{z^5-z^2}{x^2+y^2+z^5}\ge0.
\]

- Collected: 2026-10-11.
- Status: `checkable`; [verified Litex proof](imo_2005_p3.lit).
- Experiment features: Higher powers and a product lower bound.
- Proof idea: For each cyclic summand, subtract (x^2-1/x)/(x^2+y^2+z^2). The difference equals (x^3-1)^2*(y^2+z^2)/(x*(x^5+y^2+z^2)*(x^2+y^2+z^2)), hence is nonnegative. Product xyz>=1 gives 1/x<=yz. Sum the three lower bounds and use x^2+y^2+z^2>=xy+yz+zx.
