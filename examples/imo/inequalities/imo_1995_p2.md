# IMO 1995 — Problem 2

Reciprocal cubic inequality.

## Problem

Let $a,b,c$ be positive real numbers satisfying $abc=1$. Prove
\[
\frac{1}{a^3(b+c)}+\frac{1}{b^3(c+a)}+\frac{1}{c^3(a+b)}\ge\frac32.
\]
