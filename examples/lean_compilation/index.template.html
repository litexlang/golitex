The role of Litex.lean
It defines the Lean meaning of Litex objects and facts, and proves the bridge laws used by generated terms. Obj keeps its value and WD evidence; membership remains a fact. The generated theorem retains an explicit M : Semantics interpretation, with its required laws.
Here, a fixed ring! check proves the recorded rational-normalization equality on native observations; sameOfDenoteNumber transports it back to the same source objects. litex_complete and litex_checked_step close and package the supplied proofs, rejecting open goals.
