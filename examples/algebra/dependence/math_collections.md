# Mathematical organization

The concrete tracer starts with a verified zero linear combination with at
least one nonzero coefficient. General dependence supplies a maximal nonzero
index and represents that vector using its preceding prefix. The caller obtains
the existential witness without guessing a specific selected index.

The general proof orders only the finite set of indices. The scalar field need
not be ordered. Restricting the partial sums, discarding a zero tail, and solving
the remaining nonzero scalar equation are separate mathematical steps.

An empty list is independent even in a zero-dimensional space; a singleton
zero vector is dependent. Dropping the nonzero-scalar, tail-zero, dependence,
or pointwise restriction premise would change the mathematical claim and must
remain rejected. No new mathematical operations or trusted premises are added.
