# Mathematical organization

A deletion retains the surviving values in the original list range. Induction
on list length then generalizes over all spanning lists at that length:
independent lists are bases already; dependent lists have shorter spanning
lists with retained values. The induction result includes m<=n and range
inclusion, rather than merely existence of an unrelated basis.

The exported finite-dimensional premise means existence of a finite spanning
list. Combining that witness with extraction proves finite basis existence.
The empty list of the singleton zero space exercises the induction base.
Using the obtained basis for unique coordinates checks a real downstream use.

Existence does not specify a unique basis, preserved order, unchanged length,
strictly shorter length for every input, or equality with the entire original
range. The corresponding false strengthenings remain rejected. All bases
having the same length, and any dimension selector, need further mathematics.
