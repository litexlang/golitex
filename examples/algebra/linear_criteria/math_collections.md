# Mathematical use chain

Rank-nullity and the zero-dimensional carrier identify injectivity from
nullity or full source rank. A full-dimensional image subspace identifies
surjectivity from full target rank. With equal finite source/target dimension,
injectivity and surjectivity therefore each imply bijectivity. A supplied
linear bijection preserves dimension.

Coordinate exchange on R2 supplies a concrete nontrivial inverse. The set-level
selector uses only bijectivity and unique preimages. Its reverse linearity
uses the forward map's injectivity, additivity and homogeneity; finite
dimension plays no part in that proof. Empty sets admit an empty inverse
function and no evaluation arguments. The singleton vector space has
dimension0 and its identity inverse evaluates at0.

All field/vector-space laws come from the existing fixtures and shared std.
No extra mapping property, finite target for an arbitrary-source inverse, or
source-finiteness guard on the selected inverse is assumed.
