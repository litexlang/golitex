# Independent and spanning list lengths

The main consumer starts from the real generators (1,2,3), extracts a finite
basis using the existing general existence theorem, and compares it with the
known unit basis. Its previously unspecified length m is forced to equal 1.
It obtains the unique natural basis length and proves that this value is 1.
The real-field/unit-basis data are reused through the existing basis context.

The zero-space boundary obtains a basis from the empty spanning list, calls
the length bound at generating length 0 and proves that its unique natural
basis length is 0. contracts.lit checks all six new theorem interfaces over
arbitrary supplied fields and vector spaces, without scalar ordering.

Run python3 examples/algebra/basis_comparison/check.py after a release build.
It checks four positive files, ten final-statement rejection controls, actual
archive/shared-data imports and complete std/WiX equality. Missing prefix,
independence, span membership, spanning, basis, basis-length or finite-dimensional
hypotheses reject. A strict length inequality and universally positive basis
length are false strengthenings and reject.

No maintained source adds trust, axiom, abstract_prop or a selected dimension
operation. Equal finite basis lengths and existence of a unique natural basis
length are proved; basis extension, dimension selection and rank-nullity remain
separate results.
