# Mathematical dependency and examples

Coordinates come from unique representation in the actual ordered source
basis. Their reconstruction/addition/scaling laws define a complete linear
map into an arbitrary supplied vector space using any target image list.
Unit coefficients certify the requested basis values and uniqueness follows
from preserving combinations. This permits dependent images as well as bases.

Two bases recover the same coefficient list in opposite directions, yielding
a bijection and a reversed construction for its selected inverse. A supplied
linear bijection carries bases to bases. Finite-dimensional existence chooses
one source basis and one target basis of their equal dimension, without
asserting uniqueness of that choice. Dimension invariance supplies necessity.

Actual repeated images distinguish a noninjective extension from the
coordinate-swap isomorphism. The empty-basis construction maps a singleton
zero subspace into the plane, and becomes bijective only when the target list
is a basis of the target zero space too. The field object is shared exactly;
no silent equality between independently supplied field structures is used.
