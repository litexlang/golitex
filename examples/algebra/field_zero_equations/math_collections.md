# Zero-coefficient field equation dependencies

Zero absorption makes `0*x` equal to zero for every field element. A nonzero
right side therefore cannot equal it. In the zero-right-side case, both field
zero and field one solve the equation, and the Field axiom makes them distinct.

For the affine case, adding `b` gives `0*x+b=b`; an unequal target is therefore
impossible. Again zero and one witness distinct solutions when the target is
`b`. No finite-field or cardinality construction is assumed.
