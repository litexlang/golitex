# Finite pushforward concepts and proof spine

The construction starts from a supplied finite nonempty probability carrier
and an actual complete unary map F into an arbitrary Target. Its data are the
actual image fn_range(F) and the real event function P(preimage_set(F,A)).
Finite image and nonemptiness follow from the source carrier and one witness;
no bijection, finite ambient target or positive atom assumption is introduced.

Native preimage_set handles events; preimage(F,y) is the point fiber.
Extensionality proves empty/whole inverse images, preservation of unions and
disjointness, and the singleton/fiber equality. Existing source probability
laws then establish the complete target probability-space contract.

The dice parity example follows the fibers {2,4,6} and {1,3,5} to two masses1/2.
Finite enumeration unfolds parity(k)=k%2 before checking its values; each fiber
also publishes its exact source-carrier inclusion. The constant map collapses
all six outcomes to7 with mass1. Identity on a Dirac source retains both image
points although point1 has probability0.

The source exports one four-parameter event-function family, one bundled-value
family and seven named theorems. This module's ten generic contracts check
those exact signatures and types without adding mathematical premises. Its
100 actual statements are independently checked without importing those
generic contracts. Fourteen controls test wrong masses and source/map/event
domains; their last statements must be the only rejected statements.

No trust, abstract proposition, new native rule/search permission or compiler
implementation is supplied here. This finite model leaves expectation
transport, random-variable independence, statistics and measure theory open.
