# Concept Inventory

| Concept | Litex form | Why it is here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| point in the plane | native `cart(R,R)` | no custom point structure |
| scalar field | `have fn` | concrete surface |
| coordinate difference quotient | `thm` | checked precursor to partial derivatives |
| coordinate partial derivative | epsilon-delta `prop` + `thm` | analytic meaning, not just a formal gradient formula |
| coordinate gradient | `prop` + `thm` | packages the two proved partial derivatives |
| gradient value | `have fn` | executable `(3,4) ↦ (6,8)` endpoint |

The first version stops before total derivatives, Jacobians, chain rules,
multiple integration, and vector calculus.
