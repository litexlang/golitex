# IMO 2001 — Problem 2

Radical inequality.

## Problem

For all positive real numbers $a,b,c$, prove
\[
\frac{a}{\sqrt{a^2+8bc}}+\frac{b}{\sqrt{b^2+8ca}}+\frac{c}{\sqrt{c^2+8ab}}\ge1.
\]
Each square root denotes the nonnegative real square root.

## Source and status

- [Official year page](https://www.imo-official.org/problems/2001/)
- [Official English problem PDF](https://www.imo-official.org/assets/documents/problems/2001/2001_eng.pdf)
- Statement restated from the official contest problem; not a shortlist problem.
- Collected: 2026-10-11.
- Status: `checkable`; [verified Litex proof](imo_2001_p2.lit).
- Experiment features: Square roots, positive denominators, and inequalities.
- Proof idea: Set r=sqrt(a^2+8bc), t=sqrt(b^2+8ca), u=sqrt(c^2+8ab). Two weighted Cauchy identities imply (sum a/r)(sum ar)>=s^2 and (sum ar)^2<=s(sum ar^2), where s=a+b+c. The cubic difference s^3-(a^3+b^3+c^3+24abc) is a weighted sum of squared differences. Thus sum ar<=s^2 and the required fraction sum is at least 1.
