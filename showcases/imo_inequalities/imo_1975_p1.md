# IMO 1975 — Problem 1

Ordered sequences and permutations.

## Problem

Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $x_1,\ldots,x_n,y_1,\ldots,y_n$ be real numbers with
\[
x_1\ge x_2\ge\cdots\ge x_n,\qquad y_1\ge y_2\ge\cdots\ge y_n.
\]
For every permutation $(z_1,\ldots,z_n)$ of $(y_1,\ldots,y_n)$, prove
\[
\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i-y_i)^2\le\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i-z_i)^2.
\]
