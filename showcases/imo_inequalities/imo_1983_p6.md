# IMO 1983 — Problem 6

Triangle-side polynomial inequality.

## Problem

Let $a,b,c$ be the side lengths of a triangle. Prove
\[
a^2b(a-b)+b^2c(b-c)+c^2a(c-a)\ge0.
\]
Determine all cases of equality.

For a later algebraic formulation, the triangle condition means
$a,b,c>0$, $a<b+c$, $b<c+a$, and $c<a+b$.
These are strict inequalities: degenerate triangles are excluded.

- Proof idea: Use six exhaustive side-order cases reduced by cyclic rotation to two nonnegative polynomial certificates. Ascending order bounds F below by the minimum side squared times two squared gaps; reverse order uses the strict triangle inequality to bound F below by the minimum side squared times the extreme gap squared. Vanishing implies all sides equal; prove the converse explicitly.
