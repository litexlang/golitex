# Lemma 1.4.7: convergence implies Cauchy

The mathematical route is the textbook epsilon/3 argument. In Litex, the
tail inequalities and triangle chain check directly. The reusable detail is
inside the existential Cauchy-tail witness: after proving its universal
pairwise bound, explicitly fold it with
`by def $is_metric_cauchy_after(...)`. Without that fold, the verifier does
not automatically use the universal fact as the named predicate.
