# Proposition 1.1.20: metric-limit uniqueness

The successful Litex route follows the textbook proof directly, but its
control shape matters. Start with `by contra` so the assumed inequality of the
two limits remains in scope. Prove positivity of their distance with a
three-way order split, choose the two epsilon tails, and use their finite-set
maximum as one common index.

The decisive inequality should be a single explicit chain from the triangle
inequality through symmetry and the two tail bounds to `dist(a,b)`. Combining
the final comparison with a strict `dist(a,b) < dist(a,b)` closes the
contradiction. This avoids the less informative nested-case composition tried
earlier.
