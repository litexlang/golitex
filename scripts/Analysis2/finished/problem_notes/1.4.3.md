# Lemma 1.4.3

source: Tao, *Analysis II*, fourth edition, Lemma 1.4.3.

problem: Every subsequence of a convergent sequence converges to the same
limit.

proof_idea: A strictly increasing map from positive integers satisfies
`phi(n) >= n`. Therefore every sufficiently late term of the subsequence is a
sufficiently late term of the original sequence.
