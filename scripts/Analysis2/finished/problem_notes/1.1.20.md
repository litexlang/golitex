# Proposition 1.1.20

source: Tao, *Analysis II*, fourth edition, Proposition 1.1.20.

problem: A sequence in a metric space has at most one limit.

proof_idea: Assuming two distinct limits `a` and `b`, their distance is
positive. Choose tail bounds for the tolerance `dist(a,b) / 3` and take the
maximum tail index. Symmetry and the triangle inequality then make
`dist(a,b)` strictly smaller than itself.
