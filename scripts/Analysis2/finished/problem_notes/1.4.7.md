# Lemma 1.4.7

source: Tao, *Analysis II*, fourth edition, Lemma 1.4.7.

problem: Every convergent metric sequence is Cauchy.

proof_idea: For a positive epsilon, choose the convergence tail within
`epsilon / 3` of the limit. The triangle inequality and symmetry show that
any two terms in this tail are within `2 * epsilon / 3`, which is strictly
less than epsilon.
