# Lemma 1.4.9

source: Tao, *Analysis II*, fourth edition, Lemma 1.4.9.

problem: A Cauchy sequence with a convergent subsequence converges to the same
point.

proof_idea: At tolerance `epsilon/3`, combine a Cauchy tail of the original
sequence with a convergence tail of the subsequence. Choose an index after both
thresholds, use that its strictly increasing source index is also sufficiently
late, then apply the triangle inequality.
