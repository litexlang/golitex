# Proposition 1.1.19

source: Tao, *Analysis II*, fourth edition, Proposition 1.1.19.

problem: In a discrete metric space, convergence to `a` is equivalent to
being eventually equal to `a`.

proof_idea: For the forward direction, use convergence with epsilon `1/2`.
Every tail distance is at most `1/2`, but a discrete distance is either zero
or one, so the tail equals `a`. Conversely, on an eventually constant tail the
distance to `a` is zero and therefore is at most every positive epsilon.
