# Corollary 1.2.11

source: Tao, *Analysis II*, fourth edition, Corollary 1.2.11.

problem: The closure of E is both the union of its interior and boundary and
the complement in X of its exterior.

proof_idea: For the first identity, use the two checked pointwise directions
between adherence and interior-or-boundary membership. For the second, a
non-adherent point has an exterior ball, while a non-exterior point has every
ball meet E. Close both inclusions by set extensionality.
