# Proposition 1.2.10

source: Tao, *Analysis II*, fourth edition, Proposition 1.2.10.

problem: Adherence, interior-or-boundary membership, and being a sequential
limit from E are equivalent.

proof_idea: An adherent point is either interior or, if not, cannot be
exterior and is therefore boundary. Conversely, an interior point itself lies
in E and hence in every ball about itself, while a boundary point has no
exterior ball. A convergent E-valued sequence supplies a late term in every
ball. The only remaining direction needs a countable point-selection
construction.
