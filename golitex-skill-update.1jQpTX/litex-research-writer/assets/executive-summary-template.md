# Litex: A Low-Barrier Formal Language for Readable Checked Mathematics and AI-Usable Formal Data

## Problem

Formalization is too expensive for much student-level, textbook-level, and dataset-scale mathematics to become checked and AI-usable.

## Insight

Not all low- and middle-level mathematical formalization may need a dependent-type-and-tactics workflow.

## Approach

Litex uses fact-oriented verification, named propositions, explicit substitutions, and readable equality chains.

## Evidence

Repository, verifier, examples, benchmark slice, CI, demo, and initial user/reviewer feedback.

## Deliverables

Verifier, web editor, open corpus, benchmark, documentation, soundness/scope note, and public examples.
