# Claims And Risk Control

Use claims like:

- Litex can check a growing set of textbook-style examples.
- Litex gives local feedback that humans and agents can use.
- Benchmark failures identify language, stdlib, infer-rule, kernel, syntax, diagnostic, and formulation gaps.

Avoid claims like:

- Litex replaces Lean.
- Litex is already sound in the same way as mature proof assistants.
- Litex can formalize all low-level mathematics cheaply.

Always disclose:

- trusted components;
- role of `trust`;
- builtin and infer rule review status;
- CI/example coverage;
- known limitations;
- next audit steps.
